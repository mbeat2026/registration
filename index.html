Welcome to M-BEAT!

By M-BEAT – Student Selection
An Initiative by
Centre for Insect Molecular Studies and Environmental Research (CIMSER)
PG & Research Department of Advanced Zoology and Biotechnology
Loyola College (Autonomous), Chennai – 34

in collaboration with Greater Chennai Corporation & AHC Foundation

Objectives

  1. To identify and eliminate mosquito breeding sources within participating college premises through regular surveillance and source reduction activities by student-led teams.
  2. To establish standard operating procedures (SOPs) for maintaining breeding-free campuses.
  3. To promote Behavioural Change Communication (BCC) activities for updating college authorities, faculty, students, and support staff on vector surveillance, source reduction, and preventive measures.
  4. To institutionalize periodic inspections, self-monitoring, and reporting mechanisms in collaboration with corporation officials.
  5. To recognize and certify colleges that consistently maintain breeding-free premises based on predefined criteria.
  6. To develop participating colleges as models to replicate this initiative across other educational institutions in Chennai.

Eligibility Criteria

  1. Only currently enrolled college students are eligible to apply.
  2. Candidates must be actively involved in M-BEAT activities.
  3. Candidates must maintain a minimum attendance.
